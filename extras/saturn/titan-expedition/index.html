Flight guide & Titan

Tilt the lander, then thrust along its heading. Let go of thrust to descend; tilt the other way and burn to brake. Seven samples can be collected in any order. One is high in the haze, and one lies beyond the landing shore.

Shift boosts during collection: start with one charge and earn one after every three samples. After the seventh sample, boost switches off. Space fires medium landing thrust immediately. After two seconds and releasing Shift, hold Shift for low thrust. Release both to coast. Touch controls work the same way; hold turn and thrust together.

Land upright on the far-shore pad, below 5 m/s vertically and 4 m/s sideways. Keep your landing feet above the methane sea. Clouds obscure the view but do no damage.

This is an authored Ligeia-inspired coast. Titan’s orange haze, hydrocarbon seas and icy bedrock inspire the scenery. The spacecraft, route, samples and weather are fictional; flight handling deliberately matches Ancient Venus’s Through the Veil, rather than simulating Titan’s gravity. Lighting uses the selected date or chosen lighting study.
