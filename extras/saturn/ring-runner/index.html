SATURN / RING RUNNER

Ride the rings

Collect 30 objects, visit A, B and C, and survive the 2½-minute survey.

◇ Water ice⬡ Dusty ice▣ Research cache⊗ Debris

Steer with ← → or A / D. Hold a direction and tap Space to jump to the next ring. Space alone hops over debris. Forward motion is automatic.

Flight guide & science

Left is outward toward A; right is inward toward C. Every jump needs a fresh press. The landing beacon shows your committed destination. A brief edge grace period allows a late jump; otherwise a recovery boost costs 25 hull. Red-marked debris also costs 25 hull. Four hits end the run. Collect consecutive objects for a score multiplier; damage resets the chain.

The rings are orbiting particles, not solid tracks. This skimmer, assisted gap transfers, compressed course, marked hazards and research caches are fiction. A/B/C ordering and dated sunlight use Saturn’s equatorial frame. No real moon is animated on the course clock. The 150-second survey does not represent an actual orbit. Collect 30 objects to charge Coalesce. Press Shift or Coalesce; a request during a jump waits for landing. Formation pauses the survey, saves a small fictional moonlet, and lets you name it. Each creation costs 30 stored objects; your survey total stays intact. Earned moonlets remain after failure, retry, score clearing and reload. Their displayed orbits are game extrapolations, including before their creation date, not historical astronomy.
