Take your time.

Space / Go: accelerate. A/D or arrows: steer. Shift / Brake: stop. S / Reverse: reverse slowly. Drag: look. Double-click: reset camera. At the boundary, R / U-turn reverses you; return within 5 seconds.

Lunar rover

Double-tap and hold Go: turbo, on ground or in air. Over 600m to the next stop: 200 mph rocket, 8s. Rocket eases off on approach. 4s local turbo · 6s recharge. Brake cancels. In air: arrows strafe; Go + direction turns; Brake descends; Brake + direction makes a tighter dive turn.

The rocket, ground grip and air controls are fictional. No rover tricks.

Scooter tricks

Double-tap Go: turbo. Double-tap grounded Brake: instant stop. J / Hop: jump. In the air, direction then Brake / F: trick; tap again to advance it. Hold Go + direction: air steering.
