The adapted asset textures/sky/gaia-diffuse.webp is distributed under the same license.
ASTRA modifications: resize to 1024 x 512, median filter and gentle blur to suppress
resolved star points, suppress the small M31 footprint, WebP compression. Displayed
at reduced brightness. The Magellanic Clouds remain part of the original panorama.
Reproducible preprocessing: tools/data/gaia-diffuse.mjs in the source repository.
The diffuse map is observational data, not a live sky image.
