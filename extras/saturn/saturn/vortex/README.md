# South Polar Vortex artwork

ASTRA milestone 3, November 13, 2026. Created with the **built-in imagegen tool**; no CLI/API fallback. These are authored cloud illustrations, not Cassini photographs or a reconstruction of dated weather. The renderer supplies moving cloud layers and date-based exposure.

Final workspace assets:

- `public/saturn/vortex/south-clearing-v1.png` — wide panorama.
- `public/saturn/vortex/south-clearing-portrait-v1.png` — portrait composition.

Scientific setting: [NASA, Looking Saturn in the Eye](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/looking-saturn-in-the-eye/). Cassini observed a southern polar eye surrounded by high clouds with lower clouds inside. This game compresses that setting into an illustrative local flight corridor. No NASA photograph was supplied to the generator or embedded in these assets. Portrait generation used the generated wide image as its reference.

Both final assets have opaque backgrounds. Originals remain in the tool's generated-images directory; the game uses only the workspace copies. The exact final prompts are below.

## Wide prompt

Create a high-quality cinematic atmospheric matte painting for ASTRA's Saturn South Polar Vortex collector game. Wide landscape panorama 3:1. The camera is within Saturn's atmosphere beside the towering cloudy eyewall of its SOUTH polar vortex, looking slightly downward across a deep open eye-like clearing. No planet seen from space, no hexagon. An enormous scalloped bank of pale cream and warm ivory clouds curves from upper left into the upper distant center, while a second lower, broken arc of turbulent amber-grey clouds frames the right and bottom edges. The CENTER should show a deep blue-grey, smoky teal lower cloud layer through the opening, with wispy veils and faraway intricate spiral cloud folds, visibly much lower than the luminous surrounding banks. The viewpoint is local to an immense atmospheric feature: scale compressed illustratively for a flight game, not a map. Balanced dimensional composition, naturally curling eyewall silhouette, richly textured fine filigree, soft cloud self-shadowing, subtle gold sun grazing the left bank. Restrained palette: pale cream, desaturated warm taupe, dark blue-grey depths; no saturated orange. Leave the central 40% open, medium-dark and uncluttered so small separately-rendered glowing capsules and a craft read clearly at several heights. Atmospheric depth and sophisticated realism comparable to a beautiful space documentary, no whiteout. Distinct from a flat golden cloud sea. NO rocky walls, terrain, ocean, water, ground, hole in ground, lava, spacecraft, station, objects, UI, lettering, labels, stars, moons or rings. Opaque image.

## Portrait prompt

Recompose this generated Saturn SOUTH polar vortex atmospheric illustration as a tall PORTRAIT 2:3 game background, 1024x1536. Preserve the beautiful cream and ivory curling eyewall, deep teal blue-grey lower cloud layer and soft grazing sunlight, sophisticated realistic turbulence and warm grey shadows. Show an immense local atmospheric eye opening, no hexagon. Compose a towering arc of detailed cloud wall along the upper LEFT 30% curving over the top, with broken ivory cloud towers on the right 25% and layered wisps in the lower quarter. Central 45% must be an open medium-dark cloudy atmospheric corridor all the way vertically, with delicate low contrast filaments and depth, suitable for small separately rendered bright capsules and a craft at several heights. Eyewall clouds near the vertical middle should frame, not fill, that lane. There is NO solid surface: everything is gas, softly sculpted volumetric weather. Not a rocky canyon or a hole in terrain. No water, sea, ground, spacecraft, objects, station, ring, moon, stars, text, HUD or borders. Preserve the original muted colors; not saturated orange. Output portrait composition, opaque.
