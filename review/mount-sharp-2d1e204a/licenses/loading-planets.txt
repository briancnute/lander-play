ASTRA loading globes

These WebP rotation strips are rendered derivatives of ASTRA's existing
src/render/globe.ts and its established surface data. They retain the same
surface colours, spherical projection and lighting; no new source imagery was
acquired. Generated at 128px, with 36 views plus a repeated first frame.

Source credits recorded in the existing renderer:
- Earth coastlines: world-atlas land-110m / Natural Earth; procedural surface,
  clouds, ocean and lighting by ASTRA.
- Mercury albedo: MESSENGER / NASA / JHUAPL-derived global mosaic.
- Moon and Mars maps: Solar System Scope / real lunar and Mars imagery.
- Venus cloud deck: Solar System Scope / NASA New Horizons mosaic.

Solar System Scope: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

The decorative three-second rotation is illustrative, not real planetary time.
