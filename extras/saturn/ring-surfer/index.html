SATURN / RING SURFER II

Ride the rings

Collect 30 objects, visit A, B and C, and survive the 2½-minute survey.

◇ Water ice⬡ Dusty ice▣ Research cache⊗ Debris

Steer with ← → or A / D. Hold a direction and tap Space to jump to the next ring. Space alone makes a low hop for raised ↑ HOP objects or debris. Forward motion is automatic.

Goals add no points. Ghosts never collide and pause with you. Best score wins; ties favor faster flights. Five courses are kept.

Flight guide & science

Left is outward toward A; right is inward toward C. Every jump needs a fresh press. Raised objects marked ↑ HOP require a jump; driving underneath misses them. The timing strip shows the safe launch window for raised ice. Line up first, release steering, then tap when HOP NOW appears. The countdown accounts for a flow surge. Release steering for a low hop. The landing beacon shows your committed destination. A brief edge grace period allows a late jump; otherwise a recovery boost costs 25 hull. Red-marked debris also costs 25 hull. Four hits end the run. Collect consecutive objects for a score multiplier; damage resets the chain.

At three B-ring forks, steer left onto the blue line for roomy ground pickups, or right onto the gold line for narrow caches, nearby debris and a raised middle cache. Choose one object at each gate; the other line never breaks your chain. Both lines give the same moon-building material. Gold caches award 120 base points instead of 40. The ring textures and collection forks are authored scenery, not a local ring forecast. The shadow follows the selected Sun direction. The rings are orbiting particles, not solid tracks. This skimmer, assisted gap transfers, compressed course, marked hazards and research caches are fiction. A/B/C ordering and dated sunlight use Saturn’s equatorial frame. No real moon is animated on the course clock. The star field is illustrative, not a sky chart. One circuit is compressed to roughly 100 seconds of play, not a real orbital period. Consecutive pickups build FLOW: every eighth pickup gives a short automatic surge. Missing a pickup resets the chain. Collect 30 objects to charge Coalesce. Press Shift or Coalesce; a request during a jump waits for landing. Formation pauses the survey, saves a small fictional moonlet, and lets you name it. Each creation costs 30 stored objects; your survey total stays intact. Earned moonlets remain after failure, retry, score clearing and reload. Their displayed orbits are game extrapolations, including before their creation date, not historical astronomy.
