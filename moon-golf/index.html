Race the ridge circuit through twelve checkpoints and two jumps. Drive, chip and putt: every ball stays where it rests. Play it again from there, or find it later on the map.

Three presses. One swing.

Press to start, press again to lock power, then press in the small impact window on the return. Power fills faster near the top, and hard shots have a narrower accuracy window. Miss the window entirely and start again.

Every shot aims at the central flag and cup. Your third press sets directional accuracy. Only clubs with enough nominal range are offered; a driver remains available for longer approach shots. A putter appears within about 61 m. The mini wedge reaches about 145 m, pitching wedge about 235 m, and driver about 1.4 km before bounce and roll. The flag on the bar shows the nominal distance load.

In flight: left/right thrusters turn the kart’s nose, with Mars-style coasting. Down or the action button fires down-thrust to land sooner. Hold the flight-speed button or Space to follow the golf ball faster.
