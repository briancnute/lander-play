Sensor mission

Five kits measure ground temperature, radiation exposure and the extremely thin exosphere. Mercury has no ordinary air temperature or breathable atmosphere.

The shaded crater is an authored site in local shadow, not a confirmed polar ice deposit. The course compresses a fictional Caloris-to-terminator journey; it is not a map or a measured habitat assessment.

MESSENGER is historical context, never a collectible or score penalty. Its real impact was near Shakespeare basin, far from this fictional route.

Sky streaks are stylized space particles, not atmospheric shooting stars. Mercury has too little atmosphere for an Earth-like meteor display.
