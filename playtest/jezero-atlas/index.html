Landing is a real place, not a relocated orb. The current history orb lives inside the small playable crop. In the expanded world, the actual landing site becomes accessible; retain the recorded route overlay and distinguish the historical touchdown from our chosen player spawn.

Discovery budget

Nine substantial site reveals: landing, Séítah, depot, ridge cluster, upper delta, Kodiak, Bright Angel, rim look-back and Falbreen.

One conditional addition: the 2022 balanced rock, only after its position and shape are established. Until then it remains a companion photograph.

Up to two red Mars-fact orbs: reserve for distinctive subjects such as MOXIE oxygen production or Martian sound. Research and approve the text before placing them. Maximum proposed total: twelve.

Activities share the world

The floor race reserve is an authored activity area, not an actual rover track. Its final loop needs a terrain pass. In exploration, the race course remains hidden; show it only during an active time trial.

Camera mode, drawing/track art, digging, atmospheric readings and jumping games remain parked. They can reuse finished terrain later without dictating today’s map.

Evidence and limits

Relief is generated from the existing 100 m overview sampling of a USGS 20 m CTX elevation model. It can explain world composition, not resolve small cliffs, traversability or individual rocks. HiRISE image resolution is not the resolution of a height model. The current Three Forks fine terrain is a separate source.

Positions use a local spherical projection centered at 77.405° E, 18.461° N, radius 3,396,190 m, matching the existing prototype. Elevation is not compressed. Region numbers identify reference anchors, not geological boundaries. Candidate camera-area anchors use preceding rover waypoints; they are not photogrammetrically solved camera poses.
