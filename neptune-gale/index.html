About

Survey Neptune’s atmosphere at a constant gravity of 11.15 m/s². Depth, pressure, winds and stations are compressed or authored for play. Rain crystals are fictional collectibles, not diamonds falling through Neptune’s visible clouds. The lower boundary is still atmosphere; cloud textures and wind sounds are artistic cues.
