# Remote-probe scene art

The six `io-flight-*` / `europa-flight-*` JPEGs are AI-generated scientific illustrations of invented near-surface sites. They are not spacecraft photos or dated astronomical reconstructions. Generated using the built-in image-generation tool, November 3, 2026. Standard, portrait and wide phone compositions are distinct; original PNGs are retained outside the public bundle.

Full prompts, source filenames, reference roles, output dimensions, and encoding details: `docs/reference/jupiter-moons/asset-prompts.md`. Scientific references: `docs/reference/jupiter-moons/research.md`. Actual collision terrain, probe and station are rendered separately by `jupiter-moons/world.ts` from the flight rules.

The four `*-globe-v1.jpg` selector disks are NASA image derivatives, documented separately in `README.md` in this directory.
