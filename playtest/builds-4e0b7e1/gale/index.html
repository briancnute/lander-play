Controls & world notes

Hold Drive to accelerate. Hold Brake to stop; release and press Reverse when stationary to back up. Steer with the arrows. M opens the map, Escape pauses.

Map travel is freely available. No missions, checkpoint gates or score requirements. Your position and visited stops save in this browser.

This is an ASTRA exploration rover with fictional upgrades, following Curiosity’s recorded journey. Terrain is measured; small rocks are representative scenery. The prototype scales terrain equally in all three dimensions to two-thirds size. The whole mountain and finale are future work.

NASA route snapshot: 25 September 2026, through sol 5021. The gold line is a navigation overlay, not a road. Independent source gaps remain unjoined.
