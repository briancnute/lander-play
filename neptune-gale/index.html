About this flight

A drone survey through Neptune’s atmosphere. Constant gravity: 11.15 m/s². Depth, pressure, wind speeds and stations are compressed or authored for play. The diamond-shaped rain is a fictional collectible, not a claim of diamonds falling through Neptune’s visible clouds. The lower boundary is still atmosphere. Layer textures and synthesized wind sounds are artistic cues; watch the layer panel for approaching pressure changes. Sound can be muted here or in Pause.
