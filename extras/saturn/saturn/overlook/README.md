# Hexagon Overlook artwork

Generated for ASTRA's Saturn milestone 2 with the **built-in imagegen tool**, November 13, 2026. No CLI/API fallback was used. These are authored atmospheric illustrations, not Cassini photographs, measured weather maps or exact seasonal cloud colors. The shared renderer adds active cloud motion and date-driven exposure; the artwork itself is a daytime composition.

Final workspace assets:

- `public/saturn/overlook/hex-clearing-v1.png` — wide panorama, 2172 × 724.
- `public/saturn/overlook/hex-clearing-portrait-v1.png` — portrait composition, 1024 × 1536.

Scientific setting reference: [NASA, Saturn's Active North Pole](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/saturns-active-north-pole/). No NASA photograph was provided to the generator or embedded in these generated assets.

## Wide prompt

Create a polished cinematic science illustration as a seamless-feeling wide landscape matte background for ASTRA, a realistic space lander game. Output wide landscape 3:1 panoramic composition, ideally 3072x1024. Scene: an imagined view hovering just above Saturn's north polar cloud tops near 78 degrees north, beside one immense bend of the hexagonal atmospheric jet. NOT a view from orbit, NOT an entire hexagon, NOT a planet disk. Camera looks slightly down across an immense layered cloud sea; distant hazy upper atmosphere fills upper third in pale desaturated warm grey, no outer space. A long ochre and champagne cloud bank recedes from left foreground towards the center distance then changes direction gently toward far right, suggesting a single 120-degree bend at an enormous scale. Scalloped turbulent stratified clouds, fine feathery cream tendrils and swirls, blue-grey teal shadow depths, restrained natural Saturn gold palette. Dense volumetric cloudscape, atmospheric perspective, sharp intricate fine turbulence in lower quarter and side banks. Keep central area around x=50%, y=45–65% calm low contrast airy haze for a tiny separately rendered lander and landing platform; no object drawn there. Elegant and scientifically inspired believable gas giant atmosphere, painterly-photoreal game art, high production quality. Daytime soft grazing light from upper left. No ground, no mountains, no rocks, no water, no rings, no moons, no stars, no spacecraft, no structures, no UI, no text, no logos. This is illustrative local weather, not a literal measured Cassini photograph.

## Portrait prompt

Reference/edit input: the generated wide panorama above, inspected before use.

Recompose this Saturn north polar cloudscape into a TALL PORTRAIT 2:3 game background, 1024x1536. Preserve the realistic sophisticated fine turbulence, desaturated champagne-gold cloud tops, dark blue-grey teal depths, hazy daylight and immense gaseous scale. Bring detailed sculpted turbulent cloud banks MUCH closer to the CENTRAL vertical lane: a gorgeous billowing golden cloud wall filling the left 40% and curving across the upper middle distance, with a receding blue-grey layered bank filling the rightmost 30%. Keep a narrow calm but textured cloud opening in the central 30% around x=50%, with subtle wisps and depth, for a separately drawn small craft and station, especially around 50–75% down from the top. Dense intricate cloud sea across bottom third. Show one gentle distant bend of Saturn’s hexagonal jet rather than an entire hexagon. Less empty featureless haze than source. No solid ground, rocks, mountains, water, spacecraft, platform, buildings, text, borders, rings, moons or stars. This is an illustrative local atmospheric view, not literal measured weather. Produce portrait composition.

Both assets use opaque backgrounds. Original generated files remain under the tool's generated-images directory; the game references only these workspace copies.
