MARS / JEZERO

Field notes

Real place, imagined expedition

Perseverance explored this delta front in 2022 and established its Three Forks sample depot in 2022–2023. Your discoveries are observations in a fictional ASTRA expedition. NASA’s cached tubes stay where they belong.

The local landforms come from USGS HiRISE elevation measurements; the distant crater terrain comes from the wider CTX elevation model. Surface colors, small rocks, a marked jump and race route are authored. Stars and lighting are illustrative.
