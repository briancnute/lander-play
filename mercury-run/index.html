Field notes

Mercury’s ice is in permanently shadowed polar craters. The polar journey and actual MESSENGER impact site belong to a later route. A blue memorial waypoint commemorates the spacecraft here; its 2015 impact occurred near Shakespeare basin, not Caloris.

This first course represents a compressed Caloris exit. Its thermal boundaries, vehicle and overdrive system are game fiction.
