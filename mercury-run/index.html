Mercury

Field notes

Mercury’s ice is in permanently shadowed polar craters. There are no confirmed ice deposits represented in this Caloris course. The polar journey and actual MESSENGER impact site belong to a later route. A blue memorial waypoint commemorates the spacecraft here; its 2015 impact occurred near Shakespeare basin, not Caloris.

This first course represents a compressed Caloris exit. Its thermal boundaries and vehicle are game fiction.
