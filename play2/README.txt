Terminator media audit — 2026-10-15
Present day: existing MESSENGER image identified as PIA19192, acquired January 20, 2015, https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/on-the-edge-3/ . The 2017 NASA archive filename is not the acquisition date.
Bombardment: no spacecraft photo is presented as ancient Mercury; this card has two slides, actual gameplay and conceptual art.
Concepts: generated with the built-in image_gen tool, saved as concept/mercury-present.jpg and concept/mercury-bombardment.jpg. Both are explicitly labeled conceptual.
Prompt (present): Wide 16:9 landscape concept illustration for ASTRA Mercury Terminator Present day. Ground-level view immersed in dense overlapping impact craters, low grazing sunlight, charcoal gray regolith, warm silver rims, black airless sky. Large foreground crater rim sweeping diagonally; craters recede into distance. Scientific cinematic concept art; no spacecraft, people, text, HUD, logos, meteors or atmosphere; distinct from Caloris hills and landing pads.
Prompt (bombardment): Wide 16:9 cinematic concept art for ASTRA Mercury Terminator Bombardment. Ancient airless Mercury, low above rugged charcoal-gray craters. Several incoming rocky impactors, large foreground incandescent impact and radial rock ejecta, two smaller distant impacts and incoming meteoroids. Low grazing solar light; artist reconstruction, not spacecraft photo. Vacuum ballistic ejecta; no smoke, clouds, text, HUD, spacecraft, people, buildings or water.
Gameplay captures: normal simulation advanced through the course in an isolated browser profile, default chase camera, HUD hidden, no generated overlays or changes to simulation/impact scheduling. Present-day capture at approximately 20 seconds.
Bombardment selected capture: large impact's foreground secondary strikes, airborne ejecta and a distant incoming meteor, approximately 12 seconds into the normal sequence. No HUD; in-world warning markers are part of gameplay. The concept depicts the requested larger foreground impact and multiple distant strikes more dramatically than the game renderer.
