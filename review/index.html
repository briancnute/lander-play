P1 · Moon Scooter: latest controls and balance

Instructions and context

Compare free roam with the two-minute run. Try a held left grab, release, then immediately start right + Trick. Try a quick double Trick for the committed move; use mixed/repeated directions with overlapping holds. Judge responsiveness, pose variety, points and landing-release timing.

Double Brake hops on ground and jolts downward in air; repeated pairs descend faster when no trick is active. Hold Brake plus left/right to redirect flight. Pure double Go starts a moving ground Manual or arms landing only when too low to launch a trick. While manualing, hold Brake to steer, release it to balance; slow to a stop, then hop or roll off a bump. Airtime ends Manual but keeps the combo; re-arm for the next landing. Judge stationary difficulty and transitions.

With ample airtime, LRLR + Trick–Trick should show the 9000's fifty full rider-and-scooter turns. Collect M then N and judge the brief filled-letter popup. The precise shared rules are in Ride mechanics.
