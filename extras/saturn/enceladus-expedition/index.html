Flight guide & Enceladus

Tilt your collector ship, then thrust along its heading. Release to coast; turn the other way and thrust to brake. You can turn and thrust together on a touchscreen.

Fly close to each geyser, before or after passing it. When the PHOTO button says IN FRAME, press Shift or tap PHOTO. The camera aims at the geyser automatically. Release and press again for another shot: holding the shutter never takes a delayed picture. A quiet geyser cannot be photographed.

The bright ice jets damage your hull. Photograph from beside them; cross during a lull or fly over the plume. There are no drawn danger boundaries. Missed one? Turn around and try again.

Pass all four geysers and collect all four photographs, then land on the ground platform beyond the last. Touch down upright, below 5 m/s down and 4 m/s across. Shift remains your camera throughout the flight.

An authored south-polar ice route inspired by Enceladus. The spacecraft, photography windows and eruption cycles are fictional. Flight acceleration is tuned for collector-style play. Selected-date lighting uses the existing local Sun model; eruption timing is gameplay, not a forecast.
