Prompt (present): Wide 16:9 landscape concept illustration for ASTRA Mercury Terminator Present day. Ground-level view immersed in dense overlapping impact craters, low grazing sunlight, charcoal gray regolith, warm silver rims, black airless sky. Large foreground crater rim sweeping diagonally; craters recede into distance. Scientific cinematic concept art; no spacecraft, people, text, HUD, logos, meteors or atmosphere; distinct from Caloris hills and landing pads.
Prompt (bombardment): Wide 16:9 cinematic concept art for ASTRA Mercury Terminator Bombardment. Ancient airless Mercury, low above rugged charcoal-gray craters. Several incoming rocky impactors, large foreground incandescent impact and radial rock ejecta, two smaller distant impacts and incoming meteoroids. Low grazing solar light; artist reconstruction, not spacecraft photo. Vacuum ballistic ejecta; no smoke, clouds, text, HUD, spacecraft, people, buildings or water.

concept/moon-ridge.jpg: Wide 16:9 cinematic scientific concept illustration for ASTRA Shackleton Connecting Ridge, lunar south pole. A narrow rugged sunlit ridge between deep shadowed crater depressions, near-horizontal grazing sunlight, silver gray regolith and black airless sky. Small plausible gold and white landing craft descending toward an unobstructed section of ridge. Low oblique view makes ridge topography clear. No Earth overhead, no atmosphere, text, labels, HUD or buildings. Distinct from flat Apollo mare terrain. Artist reconstruction, not survey photograph.
concept/moon-tranquility.jpg: Wide 16:9 cinematic scientific concept illustration for ASTRA Tranquility Base. Broad gently undulating gray lunar mare, fine regolith and scattered small rocks, Apollo 11 lunar module and a few footprints in middle distance, black airless sky, strong sunlight and sharp shadows. Low surface viewpoint, recognizably calm open landing site, no dramatic mountains, no craters like a skate park, no scooter, no UI, no lettering. Artist reconstruction of Apollo landing-site setting, no fanciful base or giant Earth.
concept/moon-golf.jpg: Wide 16:9 cinematic game concept illustration for ASTRA Lunar Golf at fictional Moonlight Playground. Astronaut in white lunar suit swinging a golf club on gray lunar regolith, a small ball visible just after contact. Clearly visible flag and cup beyond a shallow crater, rolling terrain gives a playful challenging golf course, black airless sky, sharp low sunlight. Wide composition with golfer at left foreground and target in middle distance. Lunar terrain not grass, no trees, no atmospheric haze, no buildings, no vehicles, no text, no trajectory graphics, no HUD. Conceptual fictional activity, not historical photograph.
