Data sources
The background image is clipped from the LRO LROC WAC monochrome global mosaic (WAC_GLOBAL_E300S0450_100M, 100m/pixel). The middle of the map is clipped from the LROC NAC high-resolution controlled mosaics (NAC_ROI_APOLLO16LOA_E090S0155, 0.9m/pixel and NAC_ROI_APOLLO16HIA_E091S0152, 0.5m/pixel) and LROC ortho-image (NAC_DTM_APOLLO16_2_M152770233_50CM, 0.5m/pixel) covering Apollo 16 landing site. The traverse line is from Phil Stooke, University of Western Ontario. It was created by tracing tracks visible in the LROC data as well as using surface photos and descriptions.
