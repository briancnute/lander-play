# Mercury approach texture

`mercury.jpg`: Solar System Scope, **Mercury 2K**, downloaded 27 September 2026.
Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

Image unmodified; mapped to a sphere with authored cinematic lighting and rotation.
The creator describes NASA imagery/elevation sources, adjusted color, and fictional
terrain filling unmapped gaps. This is visual reference, not an exact reconstructed
Caloris approach or a source for geographical measurements. On-screen attribution
appears during the approach. No historical V1 rendering or cinematic code is reused.
