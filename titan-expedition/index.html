Flight guide & Titan

Turn with ← → or A/D. Hold Space for main thrust and Shift for gentle precision thrust. Release both to coast. Touch controls work the same way; hold turn and thrust together.

Collect seven cyan sample beacons in any order. Dip into two methane inlets, climb over the ice ridge, and finish at the survey station on the right. Short burns go a long way in low gravity; dense air gradually slows your drift. Start braking before the target.

Land upright at the survey station below 2.5 m/s vertically and 2 m/s sideways. You can rest on either pad; the survey only completes after all seven samples. Keep your feet clear of the liquid and rough terrain.

Titan gravity is 1.352 m/s². Near-surface air uses 1.467 bar and 94 K, with nitrogen-dominated density and speed-dependent drag. These are representative Titan conditions, not a live weather forecast. The lander mass, engine, route and samples are designed for play. This polar inlet landscape is inspired by Titan’s lakes and icy shores, not a mapped landing site. Dated lighting controls the diffuse haze; Saturn and stars are hidden.
