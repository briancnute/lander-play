CONTENT / SCALE / CONSTRUCTION

The route carries the main story.

Along the rover journey

Concentrate recognizable geology, mission stories, photo viewpoints and meaningful discoveries along the actual route. Preserve its loops and revisits; it is not a straight road across the map.

Keep the broader world open. The route is a historical guide, not a locked campaign or required order. Existing terrain and six discoveries remain available immediately.

Off the route

Scatter a few optional activity pockets with room to approach, play and leave. Surround them with quieter terrain. These letters reserve space for ideas already documented, not six newly approved games.

Turbo distribution

Main route: occasional pickups, keeping landmarks clear.
Side trips: more pickups near activity approaches and useful detours.
Far reaches: richest availability, with outward and return chains.

Turbo rewards roaming; it must not be required to escape a pocket. Preserve the 2.5× kit and unpowered airborne coasting. Density, respawn rules and exact placement await driving tests.

Candidate activity pockets

The existing Delta race stays in its current terrain. New activity names describe game ideas, not historical Perseverance operations. Photography is also useful at main-route vistas.
