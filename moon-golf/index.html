YOUR FIRST LUNAR ROUND

Find your touch.

Drive: use the thumb stick, WASD or arrow keys. Reach a glowing tee and choose Tee off.

Swing: choose a club and aim, then pull down on the white ball. More pull means more power. Release to hit; drag above the ball to cancel. On a keyboard, hold Space to build power and release.

The dotted arc shows your flight. The ring marks the first landing, so leave room for bounce and roll. Three fresh balls per tee; your closest final distance counts.

Short swings are useful here. Try the first tee at around 35% with the sand wedge.
THE REAL MOON / OUR LITTLE FICTION

A playground at Tranquility.

This is an authored course inspired by the Apollo 11 setting, not a surveyed reconstruction. Tees, kart, terrain details and the standing target flag are game fiction.

Ball flight uses lunar gravity with no air resistance. Club launch speeds are an amateur calibration, not measured shots in a pressure suit. Dust bounce and roll are approximations. Earth carry figures are reference targets, not distances to multiply by six.

Apollo 11 brought the first people here. Alan Shepard actually played golf on Apollo 14 at Fra Mauro. Our outing imagines a later visit.
