ASTRA / VENUS / LIGHTNING SHAPE STUDY

One warning language. Five strikes.

The same Mercury bombardment red for every warning. Filled bars blink before the lightning arrives. Every dangerous branch stays inside the red footprint; the lightning alone is the hit zone.

01 / COLUMN

Lightning column

A long, restless bolt that divides the view.

A narrow lane; asks for a sideways move.
02 / FORK

Forked strike

A trunk that splits into two jagged arms.

A Y-shaped warning footprint, not a giant rectangular blockade.
03 / CHAIN

Chained strike

Three connected bolts fire in quick succession.

Every link is warned up front. The sequence remains inside that footprint.
04 / TWIN

Twin curtains

Two separated, parallel bolts with a clear central gap.

The gap is visibly unmarked; this shape should be occasional.
05 / FAN

Branching fan

A broad trunk splinters into an electrical canopy.

The dramatic rare formation; keep it away from the critical orb approach.

Proposed mix: columns and forks most often; chains and twins less often; fans rare. Actual frequencies and escape routes need gameplay testing. The once-per-run turbo ambush remains a separate encounter rule that can use one of these shapes.

These are animated design examples, not a gameplay update. Warning tint: #ff2929, matching mercury-run/terminatorImpacts.ts. Lead time is 1.4 seconds with three broad blinks; reduced-motion visitors start paused. The chain demo uses three short strikes with 0.16-second spacing.
