Your lander is home base. Take the folding scooter to the teal tee marker; the golden light marks the American flag and golf cup.

Take one golf swing and land one scooter trick. Then return to the lander whenever you’re ready for Mars. Explore at your own pace, or start an optional golf time trial after your first shot.

W / ↑ / Space: Go · A D / ← →: steer · Shift / ↓: Special · S: reverse · E: golf
Once clearly airborne, tap direction + Go for mirrored tailwhips, or direction + Special for mirrored spins. Both arrows + Go/Special: back/front flip. Double-tap Special without arrows, then hold to descend until release.

Left/right + Special: mirrored 360 spins.
Both arrows + Go: backflip · Both arrows + Special: front flip.

Tricks require 1.3 m of clear ground clearance and a fresh combination. Release a combination before starting another, and finish it before touching down. Two broad ridges on the way to the tee provide deliberate jump opportunities. Special brakes on the ground. In the air, double-tap it and hold the second press to fire downward-assist jets until release. Adding steering during the hold never triggers a trick. Double-tapping Go does nothing special.

Three clicks. One swing.

Start, lock power, then strike on the return. The accuracy target appears after you lock power. A softer shot moves quickly through the rest of the animation with a larger accuracy target. Miss the third click and the shot still goes—with a bad mishit.

Golf anywhere, or ride to the distant tee. Unholed balls stay playable. Putting scales automatically to short, middle or long distance. After a first shot, choose Time trial to ride to the ball; golf opens automatically nearby. The clock runs through riding and shots, pauses in menus, and stops at the cup. Stroke and time bests are tracked separately. Putting is a forgiving game approximation: real lunar regolith is rough and powdery, not a grass green.
