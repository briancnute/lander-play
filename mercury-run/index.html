PROJECT ASTRA / MERCURY

Ride the edge of daylight.

Pilot a fast remote rover out of Caloris. Reach the rendezvous in four minutes; facts improve your score but are not required to finish.

Darkness to your left. Sunlight to your right. Cross either edge briefly, then return to the middle to shed thermal load. Dodge crater bowls and commit to your jumps: air steering is limited. Three gold beacons and an unmarked discovery hold four facts. Each missed fact costs 180 points; each crater fall costs 20. Faster runs score better.

Mercury

Field notes

Mercury’s ice is in permanently shadowed polar craters. There are no confirmed ice deposits represented in this Caloris course. The polar journey and actual MESSENGER impact site belong to a later route. An unmarked debris discovery interprets MESSENGER as a game Easter egg; its actual 2015 impact occurred near Shakespeare basin, not Caloris.

This course represents a compressed Caloris exit. Its thermal boundaries, remote rover and launchable ejecta ridge are authored game fiction.
